Account Deletion

FLOPly — Poker Training App Android

To delete your FLOPly account, open the app → tap your avatar or profile → go to Account Settings → tap Delete Account. Your account and all associated cloud data will be permanently removed.

01 How to Request Account Deletion

FLOPly provides an in-app account deletion option that is available to all signed-in users. You do not need to contact support to delete your account — you can complete the process entirely within the app.

Steps to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the FLOPly app on your Android device.
  2. Make sure you are signed in to the account you want to delete.
  3. Tap your avatar or profile icon (top area of the home screen).
  4. Navigate to Account Settings.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

The deletion is permanent and immediate. Once confirmed, your account cannot be recovered.

Alternative: request by email

If you cannot access the app, you may submit an account deletion request by email to: [COMPLETAR: email de contacto]. Please include the email address associated with your FLOPly account (the Google account you used to sign in). We will process your request and confirm deletion within a reasonable timeframe.

02 Data Deleted

When you delete your account (whether through the app or by email request), the following data is permanently and immediately deleted:

  • FLOPly account profile — your display name, email address, profile photo URL, country, locale, account creation date, and all linked sign-in providers (e.g., your Google account association). Stored in: Firestore users/{uid}.
  • Academy progress — all completed lessons, unlocked levels, concept mastery records, and exercise history stored in the cloud. Stored in: Firestore users/{uid}/academy/progress.
  • Firebase Authentication account — your sign-in identity is permanently deleted from Firebase Authentication. You will no longer be able to sign in with the same account.

03 Data Retained After Deletion

The following data is not deleted when you delete your FLOPly account, for the reasons described below:

Promo code redemption records

If you redeemed a promotional code while your account was active, a record of that redemption (containing your user ID and the redeemed code) is stored in our database (promo_redemptions collection). These records are created by our backend system and cannot be deleted through the in-app deletion flow. They are retained to prevent abuse of promotional codes (e.g., preventing the same code from being redeemed multiple times by creating and deleting accounts). If you wish to request deletion of these records, please contact us at [COMPLETAR: email de contacto].

RevenueCat purchase history

FLOPly uses RevenueCat to process in-app subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat retains your purchase history (subscription transactions, purchase dates, and renewal records) independently of your FLOPly account, in accordance with RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Deleting your FLOPly account does not automatically delete your data from RevenueCat's servers. To request deletion of your RevenueCat data, you must contact RevenueCat directly.

Local data on your device

FLOPly stores some data locally on your device to support offline functionality. This local data is not transmitted to our servers and is not linked to your account identity:

  • FLOPly Coins balance and ad history (floply_coins_economy.json)
  • Daily training quota usage (floply_training_quota.json)
  • Saved poker ranges (saved_ranges.json)
  • App UI preferences, such as dismissed tooltips (floply_prefs.json, tool_explainers_dismissed.json)
  • A local copy of Academy progress (used as cache for offline access)

This local data is automatically removed when you uninstall FLOPly from your device. It is not accessible to FLOPly after your account is deleted, as it exists only on your device.

04 Active Subscriptions

Deleting your FLOPly account does not automatically cancel any active Google Play subscription.

If you have an active FLOPly Premium subscription, you must cancel it separately through the Google Play Store to stop future charges. Deleting your in-app account will remove your access to Premium features, but the subscription billing through Google Play is managed independently.

To cancel your subscription:

  1. Open the Google Play Store on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Find FLOPly and tap Cancel subscription.

Refund requests for subscription payments must be directed to Google Play's refund policy.

05 Google Account Data

FLOPly uses Google Sign-In for authentication. Signing in with Google grants FLOPly read access to your Google account's name, email address, and profile picture for display purposes only. Deleting your FLOPly account removes this association from our systems, but does not affect your Google account itself.

You can review and revoke FLOPly's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
